# Feedback: rename the `Items` section of the plan template

## What I want

I want the `Items` section of the plan template to be called something else.

`## Items` is the section that lists the other items a plan is carried out
through, and as a heading it says too little. Arriving at the section, the
only thing that suggests what goes in it is the comment underneath, and a
comment is the part a reader sees after they have already decided the section
is not for them. `Items` could just as well hold the steps, the files to
touch, or the things left out.

Something that names the relation, such as `Related items` or `Backlog
items`, would say what belongs there before the comment is read. I do not
have a strong preference for which.

What turned out to matter is not the heading but the lines under it. The
template asks for one item per line, by slug, and a few slugs alone do not
say how they relate: which one has to come first, which one waits, or which
run side by side. Whatever the section is called, the line has to carry the
relation.

## Outcome

Reshaped and accepted: `Items` becomes `Related items`, and the line under it
carries the relation rather than a slug alone.

The heading is the smaller half. `Items` becomes `Related items`, but what
answers the complaint is the line under it: the template asks for an item and
how it relates to this plan, rather than for a slug alone, and leaves the
words for the relation to the writer. Fixing a vocabulary (`carries out`,
`waits on`, `runs in parallel`) would turn the section into a field to fill
in, and the relations worth writing are the ones nobody thought to list.

A heading that names the relation still earns its place: `Related items`
says what the section is for before the comment under it is read, and the
comment is what a reader sees after deciding the section is not for them.
